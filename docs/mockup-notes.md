## Visual language

- **Palette:** warm cream/off-white background; tomato red for primary buttons, the italic accent word, and the CTA band; deep green for the stats band, tags, and bullets; pale sage and blush pink for soft blob shapes and card tints.
- **Type:**
  - Headings: a high-contrast serif (looks like Fraunces or something similar).
  - Accent words in headings (*vit bien.*, *rejoindre.*, *tous les âges.*, *Dt.P.*): red italic serif with a thin green underline swoosh.
  - Body: a clean sans-serif.
  - Logo and small kickers above the CTAs (*Un projet pour votre groupe ?*): a script face, like the current site's Dancing Script.
- **Eyebrow labels:** small-caps green text with a leaf glyph, e.g. `🌿 PROFIL`, `SERVICES`, `AVEC QUI JE TRAVAILLE`.
- **Illustrations:** flat food icons (tomato, lime slice, yellow pepper, leaves, sprout, apple, red pot, five spice dots), plus large soft circles in the corners.
- **Components:**
  - Rounded cards with a hairline border.
  - Pill tags and pill chips with a green dot.
  - Numbered section markers (`01`–`04`) in large pale-pink serif.
  - Vertical timeline with red dots and a dashed green line.
  - Stat grid on green with a dotted texture.
  - Pull-quote with a red left border, and a big red `«` quote mark.
- **Header:** script logo; nav where the active page sits in a sage pill; an FR/EN segmented toggle (active = green); a round icon button that is probably a dark-mode toggle.
- **Every page ends with** a red CTA band (script kicker, serif headline, a white "Écrivez-moi" button, and a second outline button), then a 4-column footer: brand and tagline / Contact / Membre / Pages, plus "Haut de page".
